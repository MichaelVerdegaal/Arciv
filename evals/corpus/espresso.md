# Espresso dialing-in log

## Fixing sour or bitter shots

Sourness means under-extraction: grind finer, raise the water temperature a
degree, or lengthen the shot. Harsh bitterness is the opposite problem —
over-extraction — so grind coarser or cut the shot shorter. Change one
variable at a time and taste before touching anything else.

## Current recipe

18 g in, 36 g out, 28 seconds on the medium roast from the local roaster.
The grinder sits at 2.4 on the dial; humidity swings move the sweet spot by
about two tenths.

## Milk texturing

Stretch for the first three seconds only, then keep the wand buried at an
angle that spins the milk. Microfoam should pour like wet paint. Purge the
wand immediately or the milk bakes into the tip.
