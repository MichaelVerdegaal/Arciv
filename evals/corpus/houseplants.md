# Houseplant care

## Watering

Overwatering kills more plants than drought: yellow lower leaves plus wet
soil means the roots are suffocating. Water only when the top two knuckles
of soil are dry, then water thoroughly until it drains. Pots without
drainage holes are decorative sleeves, not homes.

## Light

"Bright indirect" means within a meter of a window but out of the direct
beam. A monstera stretching long bare stems is begging for more light;
crispy bleached patches mean too much direct sun.

## Pests

Fungus gnats breed in constantly damp topsoil — let it dry out and they
leave. Spider mites announce themselves with fine webbing under leaves;
shower the plant and raise the humidity. Wipe leaves monthly so you notice
problems early.
