# Freelance tax notes

## Deadlines

Quarterly estimated payments are due mid-April, mid-June, mid-September,
and mid-January. The annual return deadline is May 1st with an automatic
extension available on request. Late quarterly payments accrue interest
from the due date, not from filing.

## Deductible expenses

Home office by floor-area share, the business laptop depreciated over three
years, software subscriptions, professional books, and the accountant's fee
itself. Meals with clients are half-deductible; the commute is not
deductible at all.

## Receipts

Photograph every receipt the day it appears and drop it in the tagged
folder; thermal paper fades to blank within a year. The bank export alone
does not satisfy an audit — the invoice or receipt must show what was
bought.
