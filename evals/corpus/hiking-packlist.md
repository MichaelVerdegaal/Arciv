# Day-hike pack list

## Clothing layers

Cotton kills in cold rain — base layers should be wool or synthetic. Pack a
fleece midlayer and a shell even when the valley forecast is sunny; summit
weather turns fast above the treeline. Spare socks weigh nothing and fix
half of all misery.

## Water and food

Plan a liter per two hours of walking, more in heat. The filter straw covers
refills from streams. Salty snacks beat sweet ones on long climbs; cramping
usually means electrolytes, not effort.

## Safety kit

Headlamp, whistle, space blanket, tape, and a paper map of the area — phones
die in the cold. Tell someone the route and the turnaround time before
leaving signal.
