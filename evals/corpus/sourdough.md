# Sourdough baking notes

## Starter care

Feed 1:5:5 at room temperature the night before baking; the starter should
double within six hours and smell yogurty, not like nail polish. A layer of
grey liquid on top just means it is hungry — stir it back in and feed.

## Handling wet dough

High-hydration dough sticks to everything until gluten develops. Wet your
hands instead of flouring them, use coil folds every half hour during bulk,
and resist adding extra flour — the dough tightens up noticeably after the
second fold.

## Proofing and scoring

Cold-proof shaped loaves overnight in the fridge; they score much cleaner
straight from the cold. Slash at a shallow angle with one confident stroke.
An underproofed loaf blows out at the score; an overproofed one spreads flat
and barely springs.
